1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Zero-Plug Monolithic Star Circuitry: Main ports (P, T, A, B) drilled vertically to midline Z=0, intersecting orthogonally with lateral G 1/4" BSPP ports, completely eliminating threaded plugs, expander balls, and leak-prone construction bores.
  • Open Half-Height Stepped Shoulders: Four corner shoulder steps machined from Z=+30mm down to Z=0.0mm with R6.0mm internal blending, providing top-down M6 bolt fastening without thin-blade edges.
  • Peripheral Pocket Weight Optimization: Four bottom-edge pockets (depth 12.00mm, R2.50mm transition, R1.50mm floor fillet) reducing total unit mass to 560g while maintaining conduit wall thickness ≥4.50mm.
  • Precision GD&T & Cleanroom Masking: Datum [A] flatness ≤0.005mm (Ra ≤ 0.4 μm) and locating pin [B] perpendicularity ≤0.008mm, with 100% masking mandate on internal passages during Type III Hard Anodizing.
