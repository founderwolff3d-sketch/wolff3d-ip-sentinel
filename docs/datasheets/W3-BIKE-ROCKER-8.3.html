Authority: Wolff3D Autonomous IP & Engineering Operations

Application Target: Mountain Bike (MTB) Full-Suspension High-Dynamic Upper Rocker Bell-Crank
Primary Material: Forged Pre-Stretched Aircraft Aluminum Alloy 7075-T651 (AMS 4045, Yield ≥ 505 MPa)

Freedom-to-Operate Status: CLEAR GREEN

1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 5-Axis CNC Subtractive Bell-Crank: Single-piece 7075-T651 aircraft aluminum linkage integrating a 3-pivot non-orthogonal bell-crank layout (P1 pivot Φ19.000mm H7 with Φ31.0 × 4.0mm dual counterbores, P2 shock mount Φ6.000mm, P3 seatstay pivot Φ15.000mm H7), eliminating glued carbon shells and multi-piece bolted joints.
  • G1 Continuous Tangent Slot-Thru Architecture: 2x through-slots with fully tangent semicircular ends (Slot 1: 12.0 × 39.93mm, R6.0mm ends; Slot 2: 10.0 × 39.05mm, R5.0mm ends) aligned with the -13.32° bending neutral axis of the P1-P2 beam, suppressing stress concentration factor Kt ≤ 1.80 and satisfying R ≥ 1.3x tool radius anti-chatter rules.
  • Anti-Ovalization Solid Ligaments: Main pivot P1 bearing boss maintains ≥8.00mm solid isolation walls to both cutout slots (9.35mm left, 8.20mm right), preventing bearing bore ovalization (ΔD ≤ 3.2 μm) under 5.4 kN rear-wheel landing dynamic impact.
  • Prior Art & Kinematic Decoupling: Single fixed-pivot bell-crank geometry independent of rear axle concentricity (decoupled from Trek ABP) and virtual instantaneous centers (decoupled from Santa Cruz VPP); fully protected as existing prior art based on the 2015 Technical University of Liberec topology optimization benchmark.
