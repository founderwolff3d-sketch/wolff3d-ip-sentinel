1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Part Consolidation & Zero Stack-Up: Single-piece 5-axis CNC subtractive mount integrating twin precision alignment pins (Φ3.000 +0.006/0.000mm h6 × height 6.00mm, true position ≤ Φ0.008mm, cylindricity ≤ 0.002mm) machined directly from the pocket floor, eliminating separate press-fit pins, press-fit bore micro-strains, and assembly tolerance stack-up.
  • Rigid Seating & Monolithic Vacuum Flow: Central component pocket (40.0 × 30.0 × depth 4.00mm) supported by an 8.00mm solid homogeneous base floor, directly integrating 2x recessed vacuum ports (Φ6.0 × 1.0mm) and Φ2.0mm straight ports, avoiding multi-layer glued channels or brazed vacuum manifolds.
  • Decoupled Datum Interface & High-Pressure Rigidity: 4x perimeter M3 counterbore fasteners maintain ≥4.50mm solid wall thickness to corner chamfers; base Datum [A] (flatness ≤ 0.005mm) and seating Datum [B] (parallelism ≤ 0.008mm) withstand 2000 N bonding force with <1.5 μm elastic deformation.
  • Class 1 Ultra-Clean & ESD Surface Engineering: 100% subtractive CNC manufacturing without SLM/SLS printing to prevent particle contamination; finished with MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodizing (20-25 μm with PTFE sealing) or ESD PEEK (10^6 - 10^9 Ω/sq) for scratch-free, non-damaging contact with optical lenses and bare dies.
