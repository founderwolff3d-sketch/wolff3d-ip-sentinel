1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Continuous Anti-Snag Profile: Monotonically sloping dorsal ridge (6.34° inclination) transitioning into an R0.30mm continuous tangent fillet and C0.18 × 45° / R0.20mm blunt bullnose tip, eliminating trocar catching and tissue laceration.
  • Flat-Seat Clevis Hinge Architecture: Half-thickness step face (1.30 ± 0.02mm, Datum [A]) with a planar ring contact area (>1.25mm perimeter margin) around the Φ0.900 H7 pivot bore, preventing non-planar pin tilting.
  • Dual-Pin Asymmetric Kinematic Interface: Kinematic coupling uniting a Φ0.900 H7 main pivot bore and an offset Φ0.700mm actuation pin bore (span 3.30mm, eccentricity 0.60mm), fully compatible with standard planar 4-bar linkage drive trains.
  • Constrained-Aspect Atraumatic Serrations: Low aspect-ratio grasping teeth (pitch 0.60mm, depth 0.15mm, top flat 0.33mm, root R ≤ 0.05mm) strictly confined to the lower bite boundary, distributing gripping pressure without cutting vascular tissue.
