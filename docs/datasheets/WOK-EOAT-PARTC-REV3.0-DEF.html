1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Direct-Mount Part Consolidation: Single-piece 6061-T6 monobloc flange eliminating separate quick-change adapters, multi-layer clamping plates, and intermediate brackets. Dual heavy-duty SMC ZP2-3050HWS oval suction pads bolt directly onto the bottom face via 2x PCD 18.00mm patterns (6x M3×0.5-6H threads), reducing stack height to a flat 16.0mm and cutting bare mass to 171.29g (-82.6% vs legacy quick-changers).
  • Monolithic Gun-Drilled Internal Pneumatics: Continuous straight Φ6.0mm gun-drilled runner traversing the 84.0mm transverse beam with ≥5.0mm uniform solid containment walls in all orthogonal directions. Unified G 1/8-28 BSPP parallel flat-face gasket ports eliminate taper-wedging, internal burrs, and exposed tubing.
  • Ultra-Low Inertia Kinematic Balancing: Tooling center of gravity positioned at (X=0.00, Y=+0.45, Z=-0.08) mm, practically coinciding with the cobot wrist rotation center. Principal moments of inertia (Ixx=48,902, Iyy=101,949, Izz=63,611 g·mm²) eliminate cantilever bending moments and joint overload alarms under 25.0 kg dynamic payload.
  • Anti-Static & Cleanroom Compatibility: Finished per MIL-A-8625F Type III Class 1 hard anodize (20-25 μm) with nickel-acetate conductive seal (surface resistivity 10^6 - 10^9 Ω/sq for ESD safety). High-pressure ultrasonic cavitation cleaning ensures pneumatic decay leakage ΔP ≤ 10 Pa over 30s at 0.6 MPa dry N2.
