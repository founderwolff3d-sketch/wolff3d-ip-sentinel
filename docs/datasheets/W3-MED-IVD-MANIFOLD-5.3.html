1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 3D Subtractive Microfluidic Network: Internal main channel (Φ1.60mm) and branch channels (Φ1.20mm) machined via high-precision 5-axis CNC gun-drilling into a single solid block, keeping total accumulated dead volume <15 μL without diffusion bonding layers.
  • Solid Isothermal Mounting Base: Full-density pocket-free planar underside (70.0 × 50.0mm, Datum [B]) with flatness ≤ 0.020mm and parallelism // | 0.020 | A, establishing a direct thermal coupling interface for TEC cooling/heating stages.
  • Direct-Mount Valve Seat with Micro O-Ring Enclosures: Three integrated valve stations equipped with concentric micro-grooves (width 1.60mm, depth 1.20mm, edge chamfer C0.10 × 45°) for static face sealing under M2.5 fastener clamping, eliminating shear friction debris.
  • Isolated Corner Fastening Anchors: Four perimeter M4 clearance and counterbore holes (Φ4.50mm / Φ8.0mm × 5.0mm) isolated by ≥3.25mm solid bulk material, preventing clamp stress from affecting channel fluidics.
