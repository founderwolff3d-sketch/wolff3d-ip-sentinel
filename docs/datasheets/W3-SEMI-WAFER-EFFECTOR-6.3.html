1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Multi-Pad Suction Design: Single-piece 5-axis CNC subtractive fork topology (12.0mm base flange stepping down to 4.0mm twin fingers) integrating 4x direct-milled suction pads (Φ10.0 × depth 1.50mm) with Φ2.0mm straight through-holes and underside face-sealed O-ring pocket (Φ8.5 × 1.20mm), eliminating brazed internal manifolds or diffusion-bonded cavity plates.
  • Static Passive Zero-Marking Layout: Twin cantilever fingers with a 34.0mm center U-slot span, providing 4-corner wide-stance distributed seating with C0.20mm pad edge chamfers (Ra ≤ 0.4 μm) to achieve zero backside strain markings and zero particulate shedding under -60 kPa vacuum hold.
  • Rigid Datum Flange & Zero-Thin-Wall: 4x M4 mounting counterbores (Φ4.50mm / Φ8.0 × 4.5mm) strictly maintain ≥4.50mm solid wall thickness to all outer edges, paired with 2x Φ4.000mm (m6) precision dowel pin holes for repeatable unibody alignment without spherical leveling joints or differential shims.
  • Class 1 ESD & Cleanroom Compatibility: Controlled surface resistivity of 10^6 - 10^9 Ω/sq for non-damaging electrostatic charge dissipation; 100% subtractive manufacturing strictly prohibits SLM/SLS printing to eliminate outgassing and micro-particle contamination in vacuum cluster tools.
