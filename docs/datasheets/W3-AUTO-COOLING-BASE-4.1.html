1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Parallel Straight-Micro-Fin Array: Uniform straight fin channels (fin thickness 1.50mm, gap 2.20mm, fin height 4.68mm) featuring a 100% 2.5D undercut-free vertical profile, eliminating particulate shedding and achieving ΔP ≤ 25 kPa at 10 L/min.
  • Symmetric Direct-Flow Manifold Plenums: Symmetric dual plenums (130.0 × 76.0 × 5.50mm) fed by Φ10.00mm thru ports, ensuring uniform hydraulic dispersion with 3-phase inverter bridge temperature variance ≤ 2.5°C.
  • Concentric Radiused O-Ring Enclosure: Continuous racetrack seal groove (width 3.00mm, depth 2.20mm) with concentric corner radii (inner R2.50mm, outer R5.50mm, bottom fillet R0.30mm) and ≥4.5mm isolation web against 10x M5 mounting bores.
  • Single-Setup 2.5D Subtractive Architecture: 100% CNC subtractive milling from homogeneous aluminum plate with bare masked conductive thermal island [A] (flatness ≤ 0.020mm, Ra ≤ 0.4 μm) and MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodizing on wetted cavities.
