1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Decoupling Ring Groove: Deep circular relief channel (Φ48.0mm ID × Φ58.0mm OD × depth 18.0mm) undercut from base Datum [A], forming a 6.0mm axial cantilever overlap against the interferometer bore shoulder; provides >99.2% isolation of bolt tightening torque and micro-creep stresses.
  • High-Precision Bore & Beam Tunnel: Central optical passage (Φ20.0mm THRU) paired with ultra-precision interferometer seating bore (Φ32.000 +0.009/-0.000 H6 × depth 16.0mm), holding cylindricity ≤ 0.003mm, perpendicularity ≤ 0.005mm to [A], and surface finish Ra ≤ 0.2 μm.
  • 3-Point Kinematic Clamping System: Top face 3x M3 clamping threads equispaced at 120° (PCD Φ40.0mm) at (40,60), (22.68,30), and (57.32,30), enforcing exact constraint kinematically without bi-stable over-constraint warping.
  • Class 1 Ultra-Clean Surface Engineering: Vacuum-stress-relieved forged alloy finished with high-phosphorus electroless nickel plating (MIL-C-26074 Class 1, 10-12% P, 5-8 μm) for optical cleanliness and particle-free service under ultra-high vacuum (UHV).
