1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 100% Subtractive CNC Architecture: Machined from solid 7075-T651 billet achieving 77.3% mass reduction (zero additive manufacturing, no SLM powder-trap cavities).
  • Dual-Domain Analytic Interfaces: Proximal Φ32.000 H7 stator bore (cylindricity ≤ 0.005mm) with PCD 25mm M3 pattern; distal Φ28.000 H7 CSF-14 harmonic flange bore with PCD 22mm pattern.
  • Decoupled Internal Wiring Conduit: Integrated horizontal straight through-channel (Φ12.00mm, center shifted to Z = -11.00mm), ensuring zero overlap with bearing floor pockets.
  • Undercut-Free High-Stiffness Pockets: Symmetric 9.5mm depth open pocketing with R2.50mm vertical corners, R1.50mm floor fillets, and ≥2.50mm continuous wall thickness, delivering 1st resonant mode f1 ≥ 2.15 kHz.
