1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Subtractive Architecture: 100% solid forged CuCrZr billet machined via precision CNC milling and deep hole drilling (Strictly zero additive manufacturing like SLM/DED).
  • Closed-Loop Embedded Fluidic Circuit: Comprises 1x front cross rail (Φ 10.0 mm) and 2x longitudinal rear feeders (Φ 10.0 mm), forming a clean orthogonal closed loop without aerodynamic surface breakouts.
  • Aerospace Flush Expansion Sealing: Lateral counterbores sealed with aerospace expansion plugs (Lee Betaplug Φ 10/12 mm), installed flush to rib surface (≤ 0.15 mm recess) with zero aerodynamic protrusion.
  • High Thermal-Hydraulic Rating: Withstands total pressure drop ≤ 0.35 bar at 15-18 L/min hydrocarbon/water-glycol flow under Mach 5-7 stagnation heating.
