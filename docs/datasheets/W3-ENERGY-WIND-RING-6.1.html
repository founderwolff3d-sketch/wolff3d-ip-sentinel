1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Skeletal Truss Stress-Relief Cavities: 16x cylindrical pocket cavities (Φ16.00mm, depth 12.00mm) circumferentially staggered at 11.25° between bolt bosses, maintaining ≥3.10mm minimum solid shear webs and transition fillets R ≥ 2.50mm.
  • Bi-Directional Staggered Counterbores: 16x Φ10.50mm bolt thru-holes on PCD 200.00mm, featuring 8x top-facing counterbores (Φ17.00mm × 10.00mm deep) and 8x bottom-facing counterbores alternating in a balanced bi-directional tension clamp configuration.
  • Reinforced Stepped Outer Shoulder: Cylindrical stepped outer perimeter (Φ240.00mm base, Φ229.00mm shoulder) ensuring ≥4.50mm (nominal 6.00mm) continuous solid steel backing behind counterbores, eliminating scallop-edge fatigue crack initiation.
  • High-Precision Pilot Bore & Offshore Marine Coating: Precision pilot locating bore Φ160.000 H7 (+0.040/-0.000mm, cylindricity ≤ 0.020mm) and ISO 10683 non-electrolytic zinc-nickel flake coating (15-20 μm) qualified for >1500h salt spray (C5-M marine rating).
