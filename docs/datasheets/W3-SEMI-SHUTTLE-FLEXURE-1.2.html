1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Clamped-Clamped 4-Bar Flexure: Single-piece subtractive CNC/WEDM compliant topology integrating twin parallel flexure blades (thickness 2.50 ± 0.02mm, span 44.00mm) rigidly anchored to the outer frame and connected via an 8.00mm solid center bridge, delivering zero-backlash, zero-friction, and zero-wear elastic guidance.
  • Notch-Free Vertical Fillet Architecture: All internal relief cutout transition corners carry smooth vertical fillets R1.50mm tangent to the flexure blades, suppressing the notch stress concentration factor Kt ≤ 1.35 and preventing fatigue crack nucleation under 30G reciprocating impact.
  • High-Rigidity Web & Orthogonal Decoupling: Central collet pilot bore (Φ12.000 +0.018/0.000mm H7, cylindricity ≤ 0.003mm) is separated from 2x M3 mounting threads by a ≥3.75mm solid isolation wall, achieving >98% bolt torque stress decoupling (δr < 0.18 μm) and parasitic out-of-plane drift Δz < 0.05 μm.
  • Class 1 Ultra-Clean Surface Engineering: 100% subtractive CNC machining strictly prohibiting SLM/SLS printing; finished with MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodizing (20-25 μm with PTFE sealing) while the central Φ12.0mm bore is 100% masked to preserve sub-micron fit, ultrasonically degreased with 18 MΩ·cm water for ISO 1 cleanroom service.
