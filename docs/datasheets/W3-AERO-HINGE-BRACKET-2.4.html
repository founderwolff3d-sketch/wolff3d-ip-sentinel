  • Monolithic Subtractive Twin-Lug Architecture: Single-piece 5-axis CNC subtractive topology integrating base plate (8.0mm thick) and twin clevis arms (14.0mm arm width, 10.0mm clevis thickness, 12.0mm U-channel gap) eliminating all structural fasteners, braze joints, or shrink-fit sleeves.
  • First-Octant Bulwark Datum [A]: Fully unpocketed planar base (65.0 × 50.0mm, Datum [A], flatness ≤ 0.010mm, Ra ≤ 0.8 μm) configured with 4x M4 perimeter counterbore anchors (Φ4.50mm / Φ8.0 × 4.5mm) decoupling structural preloads from clevis alignment.
