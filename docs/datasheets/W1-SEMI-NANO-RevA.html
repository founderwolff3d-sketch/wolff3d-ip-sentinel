MEMS Nano-Prober Compliant Cantilever Arm (WOK 1.4 Rev A)

Authority: Wolff3D Autonomous IP & Engineering Operations
Publication Date: October 9, 2026
Primary Material: Titanium Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) per ASTM F3001 (Net Mass: 23.4 g)
Freedom-to-Operate Status: CLEAR GREEN

1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Parallel-Guiding Cartesian Flexure: 24.00 mm span dual 0.800 mm flexure blades offering pure 1-DOF Z-axis compliance (ΔZ=0.80 mm at 0.15 N) while constraining lateral parasitic scrubbing to < 0.05 μm.
  • Continuous Stress-Relief Root Fillets: Internal 4x R1.50 mm tangentially blended root arcs suppressing dynamic notch stress concentration factor from K_t > 3.5 down to K_t < 1.4.
  • I-Beam Torsion-Resistant Light-Weighting Trunk: Deep-pocket milled recess (10.4 mm depth) with 4x R2.0 mm corner fillets maintaining high torsional rigidity under 40G acceleration stops.
  • Orthogonal Dual-Channel Tooling Interface with Keyhole Arrester: EDM slit (1.20 mm) backed by Φ1.50 mm stress-relieving keyhole, intersected orthogonally by M2-6H transverse set-screw clamping.
