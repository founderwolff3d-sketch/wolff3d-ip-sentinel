1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Double-Compound Parallelogram Flexure: Orthogonal dual-parallel guidance topology eliminating mechanical rolling/sliding bearings, pivot pins, and lubricant chambers, achieving pure solid-state elastic translation.
  • Monolithic Single-Piece Additive Growth: 100% monolithic Ti-6Al-4V ELI (HIP densified >99.98%) unifying 16x flexure blades, 4x shuttles, and inner stage, eliminating bolted leaf spring packs and 40G micro-friction slip.
  • Sub-Micron Wafer Chuck Coplanarity: Datum A center chuck contact surface flat ≤0.002 mm with Ra ≤0.1 μm, coupled with Datum B 4.000 H7 alignment pin holes.
  • High-Dynamics & Ultra-High Vacuum Compliance: First out-of-plane resonant mode f1 = 4.62 kHz with settling time ≤1.34 ms under 40G peak shock pulses.
