1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Cross-Spoke Radial Wheel Topology: One-piece monolithic elastic body uniting Φ60mm outer mounting ring with central hub via 4 orthogonal radial spokes, eliminating micro-slip and hysteresis (≤ 0.05% FS).
  • Bi-Symmetric Recessed Sensitive Beams: 4.00 mm spoke width with 8.00 mm sensitive thickness strictly symmetrical about neutral plane Z=8.00 mm, naturally suppressing vertical-to-moment cross-axis coupling drift to ≤ 0.50% FS.
  • 16x Continuous Notch-Free Root Fillets: Internal R1.50 mm tangential blend arcs at all inner-hub and outer-flange junctions, suppressing stress concentration factor to K_t < 1.35 and sustaining 300% overload without plastic yield.
  • Orthogonal 6-DOF Decoupled Strain Gauge Bridges: 16 dedicated gauge bonding sites supporting standard STC-11 thermal self-compensating strain gauges generating 1000 ± 200 με at rated capacity.
