1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Face-Sealed Monolithic Conformal Cooling Gallery: Underside open annular channel (ID Φ54.0mm × OD Φ66.0mm × depth 8.00mm) directly wetted and face-sealed against the stator casing, eliminating FSW welding seams, brazed covers, and thermal distortion of the bearing bore.
  • First-Octant Decoupled Anchor System: 4x M6 main housing through-bolt counterbores situated at the outer corners (center-to-bearing distance 49.5mm), physically isolating casing preloads to keep radial bearing bore strain <1.5 μm.
  • High-Coaxial Resolver & Bearing Alignment Datums: Precision bearing bore (Φ47.000 +0.016/-0.000 H7, depth 16.0mm, cylindricity ≤ 0.005mm) perpendicular to stator datum [A] within 0.008mm, with 4x M3 resolver bolt holes true position ≤ Φ0.05mm ensuring resolver angular drift <0.1° electrical at 20,000+ RPM.
  • Anti-Glycol & Anti-Cavitation Surface Treatment: Full cooling gallery and wetted surfaces protected via MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodizing (20-25 μm thick) to withstand 800V common-mode shaft currents and high-velocity 50/50 water-glycol erosion.
