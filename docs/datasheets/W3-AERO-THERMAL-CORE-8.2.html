1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 515nm Green-Laser Additive Body: Single-piece green-laser PBF-LB/M printed CuCr1Zr core achieving optical absorptivity ≥ 70% to eliminate reflectivity spatter and keyhole voids, avoiding multi-layer diffusion-bonded plates, welded jackets, and electroformed shells.
  • 45° Self-Supporting Teardrop Conformal Channels: Conformal internal cooling passages (Dh = 12.00mm, R6.00mm floor, 45° self-supporting apex tangent arch, total apex angle 70.53°) enabling 100% support-free additive build along +Z axis, paired with 4x Φ4.50mm straight drain/depowdering ports for complete powder evacuation.
  • High-Pressure Conformal C-Ring Land & Heavy Containment Wall: 4x C-Ring seal counterbores (Φ28.00 +0.08/0.00 × depth 3.20mm, Ra ≤ 0.4 μm, true position ≤ Φ0.03mm) with helium leak rate < 1.0E-9 mbar·L/s, reinforced by 44.0mm solid containment walls resisting 25.0 MPa proof burst pressure (safety factor np = 17.5).
  • Mode 5 Twin-Dataset & Post-HIP AFM Conditioning: Dual-STEP coordination linking Near-Net additive blank (+0.80mm machining envelope and 3x sacrificial touch-probe datum pads) to Final 5-axis CNC finish, combined with 900°C / 100-150 MPa HIP (density > 99.95%, thermal conductivity ≥ 305 W/m·K) and Abrasive Flow Machining (AFM) reducing inner wall roughness to Ra ≤ 1.0 μm.
