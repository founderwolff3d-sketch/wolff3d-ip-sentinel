1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic High-Rigidity Suspension Mount: Single-piece 5-axis subtractive block integrating a central precision guide bore (Φ16.000 +0.008/-0.000 H6) and upper collet counterbore (Φ24.00 × depth 6.00mm), eliminating piezo tilting stacks, multi-joint flexures, and elastomer damper interfaces.
  • Coaxial Stiffening & Weight Reduction Pockets: Twin symmetric weight reduction pockets (8.0 × 32.0 × depth 18.00mm) achieving >40% mass reduction, with 4x M3 counterbore fasteners strictly aligned coaxially along X=10.0mm and X=50.0mm planes; retains ≥4.00mm homogeneous solid wall to the central bore and 8.00mm solid base bulk.
  • Decoupled Rigid Datum Interface: Primary mounting base Datum [A] precision ground to flatness ≤ 0.005mm (Ra ≤ 0.4 μm) for direct load-cell seating; guide bore [B] maintains perpendicularity ≤ 0.005mm and cylindricity ≤ 0.002mm (Ra ≤ 0.2 μm) preventing wedge jamming and holding angular drift <1.0 μrad under >20G impact.
  • Class 1 Ultra-Clean Surface Engineering: 100% subtractive CNC manufacturing without SLM/SLS printing; titanium finished with micro-arc oxidation (MAO 3-5 μm) or high-phosphorus electroless nickel (5 μm) ensuring surface hardness >60 HRC, zero particulate shedding, and ISO 1 cleanroom compliance.
