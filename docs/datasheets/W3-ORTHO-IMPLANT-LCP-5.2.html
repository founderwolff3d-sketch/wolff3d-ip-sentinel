1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Centripetal Mirrored Combi-Hole Topology: Integrated 4x combi-holes featuring a 10° conical locking seat (Φ2.70mm) and dynamic compression unit slot (width 2.40mm, center span 1.30mm), symmetrically mirrored toward the central fracture datum [B].
  • Limited-Contact Flutes Undersurface: Three transverse concave cylindrical flutes (depth 0.22mm, R7.00mm at X = -9, 0, +9mm) reducing bone-plate contact area by 50-60% to protect periosteal microvascular supply.
  • Low-Profile Dorsal Crown: Transverse R8.00mm convex cylindrical upper surface with continuous lateral edge blending (R ≥ 0.40mm) and waist narrowing (5.60mm waist vs 6.50mm hole lobe), minimizing soft tissue and extensor tendon irritation.
  • Atraumatic MIPO Chisel Tips: Symmetrically tapered lead-in ends (tip width 3.80mm, thickness tapered from 1.55mm to 0.75mm with 4x 0.30 × 45° chamfers) facilitating subperiosteal subcutaneous tunneling.
