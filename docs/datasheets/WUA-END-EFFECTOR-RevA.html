1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Additive Titanium Architecture: 100% single-piece solid Ti-6Al-4V ELI eliminating CFRP epoxy outgassing, welded seams, and mechanical pivot leveling mechanisms.
  • I-Beam Rib & Conduit Co-Design: 4x dual-recess milled pockets (75.0x5.0x1.8mm) flanking a central 6.0mm structural spine enclosing a Φ1.50mm airtight vacuum line with ≥1.25mm isolation wall.
  • Sub-Micron 3-Point Vacuum Pad Coplanarity: Datum A coplanarity ≤ 0.003 mm with Ra ≤ 0.2 μm, locking wafer interface without adjustable leveling joints.
  • High-G Dynamic Response: First natural bending mode f1 = 3.12 kHz with transient settling time ≤ 0.88 ms under 15G acceleration stops.
