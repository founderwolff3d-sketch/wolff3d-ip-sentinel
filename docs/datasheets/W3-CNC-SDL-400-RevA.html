1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 1-Setup Reduction Milling: 100% single-piece solid 7075-T651 aerospace plate machined via 3-axis high-speed CNC (no closed casting cavities, no welded box structures, zero lateral undercuts).
  • Structured Honeycomb Pocket Matrix: Monocoque infill operator generating 10-cavity vertical through-pockets with R6.00mm vertical corner reliefs and R3.00mm floor fillets to eliminate stress concentrations.
  • Rigid Central Hub Domain: Solid central ring ( = 55.00\text{ mm}$) fully enveloping the $\Phi 60.00\text{ H7}$ ball-screw bore and \times \Phi 6.5$ PCD 80mm bolt circle, ensuring zero rigidity drop in transmission path.
  • Precision Guide Step Interfaces: Dual symmetrical lateral mounting steps (.00\text{ mm}$ depth, 0.00\text{ mm}$ width) with flatness ≤0.008 mm and \le 0.4\,\mu\text{m}$.
