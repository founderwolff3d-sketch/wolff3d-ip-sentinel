1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Watertight Internal Conduit Architecture: 100% single-piece solid eliminating multi-block stack seams, external cross-drilling ports, and welded plugs, mitigating vacuum virtual-leak mechanisms.
  • Dual-Domain Co-Design Synthesis: Combines a precision 1-setup machined vacuum flange interface (Datum A flatness ≤ 0.005 mm, C-seal groove) with an SLM self-supporting 4-branch manifold network.
  • Spherical Elbow Recesses with Zero Dead-Volume: R3.0 mm spherical smooth transitions eliminate stagnation corners, ensuring purge clearing times ≤ 0.85s (N2 flush to < 1 ppm SF6).
  • Semi F57 & ISO 8015 Compliant Hard Anodization: MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodized layer (35 ± 5 μm) sealed with >18 MΩ·cm ultra-pure DI water.
