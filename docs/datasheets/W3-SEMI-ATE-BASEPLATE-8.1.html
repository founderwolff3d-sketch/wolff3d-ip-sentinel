1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic 3-Axis Subtractive Honeycomb: Single-piece 7075-T651 plate (600 × 450 × 45mm) pocketed with 2.5D orthogonal honeycomb cavities (depth 30mm, 15mm continuous solid base, 10.0mm webs) delivering high structural self-damping without multi-layer laminates or elastomer dampening pads.
  • Mode 1 Zero-Undercut & Anti-Chatter Geometry: 100% straight vertical 3-axis CNC milling with zero undercuts and zero sloped holes; pocket vertical corners carry uniform R8.00mm fillets (≥1.33x tool radius of Φ12mm endmill) and aspect ratio 3:1 webs, eliminating chatter and chip recutting.
  • Center Probe Card Aperture & Rigid Grid: Central Φ120.00mm through-aperture surrounded by a 45.0mm wide solid load-bearing hub, paired with 4x Φ10.000mm H7 dowel bores (true position ≤ Φ0.02mm) and 14x M8 perimeter counterbores for purely unyielding alignment without micro-positioning flexures or eccentric cams.
  • 50/50 Anodize Growth Pre-Compensation: Finished per MIL-A-8625F Type III (30 μm hard anodize with hydrothermal PTFE sealing, ≥450 HV); CAM pre-machining bore offset (+0.030mm) perfectly restores the Φ10.000mm H7 tolerance post-oxidation without secondary reaming.
