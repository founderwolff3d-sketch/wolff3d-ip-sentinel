1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Zero-Weld Solid Flow Topology: Full CNC gun-drilled flow passages (Φ4.00mm main bore × Φ3.00mm branch ports) cross-drilled directly in a single forged block, completely eliminating welded sealing plugs and threaded blind port failure modes.
  • Continuous Solid Mounting Datum [A]: Underside features an unpocketed full-density mounting face (80.0 × 60.0mm, flatness ≤ 0.015mm) maintaining an 18.00mm centerline bulk barrier to eliminate pulse deflection under 350 bar cycling.
  • Direct-Mount Metallic Seal Pockets: Dual top-facing valve mounting stations with precision pockets (Φ14.00mm × depth 2.50mm, floor flatness ≤ 0.008mm, Ra ≤ 0.4 μm) configured for direct sealing via metallic C-rings or crush washers.
  • Isolated High-Preload Corner Anchors: 4x M6 corner through-bolt counterbores spaced ≥6.70mm from all internal pressurized passages, isolating structural clamping preload from valve sealing faces.
