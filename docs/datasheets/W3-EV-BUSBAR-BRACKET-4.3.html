1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Dual-Rail Slots & Solid Creepage Barrier: Dual recessed busbar channels (14.0mm wide × 6.0mm deep) separated by an integral 6.00mm wide solid metal isolation ridge; surface micro-arc oxidation yields >18.00mm creepage distance (exceeding IEC 60664-1 14.0mm requirement for 800V).
  • Face-Sealed Cooling Pocket with CNC Fillets: Underside open rectangular cooling pocket (70.0 × 22.0 × depth 7.00mm) machined directly below the busbar paths with R3.0mm vertical fillets and R1.0mm floor perimeter blend, maintaining 12.00mm solid conductive bulk and eliminating brazed tube assemblies.
  • Decoupled Anchor & Clamping Grid: 4x M6 perimeter mounting counterbores (90.0mm long span) physically isolated from busbar paths; 4x M4 busbar blind clamp holes retain ≥4.00mm solid wall to the water pocket, preventing coolant rupture under 10kA peak short-circuit electrodynamic repulsion (850 N).
  • High-Dielectric PEO/MAO Ceramic Layer: In-situ grown α-Al2O3 ceramic dielectric coating (25 ± 3 μm) delivering dielectric breakdown >3000V DC (leakage current <0.5 mA) and continuous corrosion resistance against water-glycol coolant.
