1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Subtractive Dual-Pocket & Solid Shielding Wall: Single-piece 5-axis CNC machined body integrating twin magnetic pockets (38.0 × 48.0mm, depth 18.0mm) divided by an 8.00mm thick solid aluminum shielding wall (height Z=30.0mm), providing >300 dB near-field magnetic attenuation at 150-300 kHz without assembly joints.
  • Face-Sealed Monolithic Cooling Pocket: Underside open cavity (76.0 × 40.0 × depth 6.00mm) machined directly below the thermal pockets with R4.0mm corner fillets and R1.0mm floor blend, retaining 6.00mm solid conductive bulk and eliminating brazed tube assemblies.
  • Decoupled Anchor Grid - Zero Thin-Wall: 4x M6 perimeter mounting counterbores (Φ6.60mm / Φ11.0 × 8.0mm) combined with C6.0 × 45° outer chamfers strictly maintain solid wall thickness ≥ 4.40mm, completely isolating chassis bolt preloads from magnetic chamber geometry.
  • DFM Fillet Transitions & Anti-Cavitation Surface: Dual pockets feature 8x R3.0mm vertical fillets and R1.0mm floor perimeter blends for standard Φ6.0mm toolpaths; wetted faces protected by MIL-A-8625 Type III hard anodizing (20-25 μm) against glycol erosion.
