1. Disclosed Technical Pillars & Defensive Scope

  • Monolithic Additive Skeletal Truss: 100% single-piece solid Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 interstage adapter unifying 6x spatial diagonal struts and top/bottom mounting flanges via SLM and HIP densification (>99.95%), eliminating composite-to-metal joints and mechanical bolt-latch mechanisms.
  • Principal Stress-Trajectory Optimization: Smooth spatial strut integration with bottom decoupling root circle (PCD 300.0mm) and top reception circle (PCD 190.0mm), avoiding stress concentrations and mounting interferences.
  • Aerospace 1-Setup Precision Machining: Bottom flange Datum A (OD 380.0mm) and top flange Datum B (OD 250.0mm) precision milled with flatness and parallelism ≤0.050 mm and Ra ≤0.8 μm.
  • High-Dynamics Launch Endurance: Withstands 15G axial compression ( = -220.7\text{ kN}$) and 3G lateral shear ({xy} = 44.1\text{ kN}$) under GEVS 14.1 {rms}$ random vibration.
