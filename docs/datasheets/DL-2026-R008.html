DL-2026-R008 — Compound Parallelogram Flexure Stage

Publication Date: 2026-11-21  |  Revision: A  |  Cleanroom Rating: ISO Class 5 / Class 100

Technical Summary: Monolithic compound parallelogram leaf-spring flexure stage engineered for piezo-driven precision positioning in cleanroom environments. Transitions from right-circular notch hinges to wire-EDM filleted leaf-spring webs (2x 1.200 ± 0.015 mm, 8x R2.50 mm, Ra ≤ 0.8 μm) to suppress out-of-plane parasitic angular tilt below 2 μrad over 100 μm stroke while keeping peak localized stress below 220 MPa under 350 N actuator preload.
